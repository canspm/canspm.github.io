# Canadian Society for Political Methodology

Welcome to the home of the **Canadian Society for Political Methodology (CSPM)**.

CSPM brings together researchers working on quantitative and methodological questions in political science across Canada. Our group supports annual meetings, methodological training, and recognition of outstanding work in the field through our awards program.
